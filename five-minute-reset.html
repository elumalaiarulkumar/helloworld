Exercise

A 5-minute reset

A simple reset you can use right where you are. No need to leave your desk.

The five steps

  1. Minute 1 60 sec

    Stop and land

    Put both feet flat on the floor and let your hands rest, unclenched, in your lap or on the desk. Say to yourself, silently: I'm pausing for five minutes. Nothing here needs me for five minutes. That's not avoidance. It's true, almost always.

  2. Minutes 2–3 2 min

    Breathe

    In 4 through your nose Hold 4 Out 6 through your mouth

    Breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of 4, hold for 4, then breathe out through your mouth for a count of 6. A longer exhale than inhale is what actually signals your nervous system to settle. Repeat for about 8 rounds.

    If your mind keeps sliding back to the thing stressing you, that's fine. Just notice it, and come back to the count.

  3. Minute 4 1 min

    Ground through your senses

    Name, in your head or out loud:

    • 3 things you can see
    • 2 things you can hear
    • 1 thing you can feel: the chair against your back, your feet on the floor, the temperature of the air

    This pulls you out of the spiral in your head and back into the room you're actually in.

  4. Minute 5 1 min

    Set one intention

    Ask yourself one question: What's the next, single thing I'll do when this minute ends? Not the whole day, just the next thing. Name it. Then go do that one thing.

A couple of notes

It works better with your eyes open and soft, not closed. That's easier to do at a desk and less jarring to come back from.

It's fine if your mind wanders the whole time. Grounding is the repeated act of noticing and returning, not staying perfectly focused.
